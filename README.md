## The entry

> This meeting could have been one line — just do it — but instead it took an hour, twelve people, and a follow-up meeting to say so

130 characters, one line, one sentence. The joke is meta: the line itself is the one line the meeting should have been — two words, "just do it" — and the punch lands on the waste it took to say them: an hour, twelve people, and a follow-up meeting. Anyone who has sat through a useless meeting recognises it instantly.
